# IT Risk Management & Governance (CENFOTEC)

This repository documents risk assessment methodologies, security governance frameworks, and performance metrics (KPIs) applied to real-world business scenarios. The objective is to bridge the gap between technical infrastructure security and organizational resilience.

---

## 1. Risk Identification & Mitigation Strategy
### Overview
Analysis of complex projects (Smart Assist) and implementation of frameworks to safeguard IT operations against technical and organizational threats.

### Technical Analysis
* **Risk Lifecycle:** Identification, evaluation, and mitigation planning for technical failures (e.g., AI integration issues) and operational bottlenecks.
* **Proactive Resilience:** Using agile practices to maintain project stability in high-complexity, distributed environments.
* **Deliverable:** [Smart Assist Risk Management Report](./Gestión%20de%20riesgos_%20Proyecto%20_Smart%20Assist_.pdf)

---

## 2. Security Governance & Policy Frameworks
### Overview
Design of information security policies aligned with international standards (ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR) and local regulations.

### Technical Analysis
* **Policy Engineering:** Creation of policies for remote work, data classification, and incident notification for hybrid physical/digital operations (Florabella S.A.).
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Implementation of controls to guarantee the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) while ensuring compliance with legal requirements.
* **Deliverable:** [Security Policies & Procedure Report](./Caso%20de%20estudio%202.pdf)

---

## 3. Security Standards & Vulnerability Awareness
### Overview
Synthesis of industry-standard security frameworks to guide technical teams and stakeholders.

### Visual Analysis & Technical Documentation
* **OWASP Top 10:** Identification and remediation strategies for the most critical web application vulnerabilities (Injection, Broken Authentication, etc.).
* **Security Standards:** Comparative analysis of ISO/IEC 27001, NIST SP 800-53, and COBIT frameworks for IT governance.
* **Deliverables:**
    * [OWASP Top 10 Infographic](./Infografía%201%20OWASP%2010.pdf)
    * [Security Standards Infographic](./Infografia%202.pdf)

---

## 4. Performance Metrics (KPIs)
### Overview
Development of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to measure efficiency in technical environments, such as microchip manufacturing.

### Engineering Takeaways
* **Data-Driven Decisions:** Transitioning from intuitive management to performance-based oversight (Production, Quality Control, HR, Logistics).
* **Metrics Implementation:** Mapping operational data to strategic objectives to identify failures before they impact production targets.
* **Deliverable:** [KPI Creation for TechChip S.A.](./Caso%20de%20estudio%203%20Creación%20de%20KPI%20para%20una%20empresa%20de%20microchips.pdf)
